Institutional Profile & Biography

Biolab

Anatomic Pathology & Diagnostic Medicine — Columbus, Ohio, USA

Executive Overview

Founded on June 15, 1995, in Columbus, Ohio, Biolab stands as a premier independent anatomic pathology laboratory dedicated to diagnostic precision, tissue-based science, and clinical clarity. Over the decades, the institution has bridged classical morphological expertise with modern automated diagnostic workflows to support physicians, surgeons, and healthcare networks across Central Ohio.

Every specimen entrusted to Biolab undergoes meticulous gross examination, histological processing, and microscopic evaluation. By pairing rigorous quality assurance protocols with rapid turnaround times, Biolab empowers clinical teams with definitive diagnostic insights that directly guide patient prognosis and targeted therapy.

Core Diagnostic Divisions

Surgical Histopathology

Comprehensive gross and microscopic evaluation of biopsy and surgical resection specimens across dermatopathology, gastrointestinal, breast, and urologic pathology.

Cytopathology

High-precision cellular diagnostics including fine-needle aspiration (FNA) evaluations, gynecologic cytology, and non-gynecologic body fluid analysis.

Immunohistochemistry (IHC)

Targeted antigen-antibody staining panels utilizing automated platforms to classify tumor lineage, identify prognostic markers, and guide precision oncology.
